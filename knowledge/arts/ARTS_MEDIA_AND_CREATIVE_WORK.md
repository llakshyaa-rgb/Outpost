# Arts, Media and Creative Work

**Coverage:** introductory guide spanning global traditions; no short guide can represent every artistic culture or school.

**Search terms:** art, arts, drawing, painting, sculpture, design, architecture, music, literature, poetry, theatre, dance, film, animation, photography, games, copyright, public domain, creative work.

## Visual arts and design
Visual arts include drawing, painting, printmaking, sculpture, textiles, ceramics, photography and digital media. Design applies creative and analytical methods to communication, products, spaces and services. Evaluate composition, form, colour, materials, context, function and audience without assuming one tradition is the universal standard.

## Architecture and built environments
Architecture responds to climate, materials, technology, culture, use, accessibility and economics. Built heritage includes ordinary buildings and infrastructure as well as celebrated monuments. Preservation decisions involve communities, conservation science, safety and changing needs.

## Music and performance
Music can be studied through rhythm, melody, harmony, timbre, form, performance and production. Traditions around the world use different tuning systems, instruments, notation and social contexts. Theatre and dance combine movement, voice, space, costume, storytelling and audience.

## Literature and storytelling
Literature includes oral traditions, epics, poetry, drama, fiction, essays and non-fiction. Interpretation can consider language, form, historical context, author, audience and translation. Oral knowledge traditions deserve recognition alongside written archives.

## Film, animation, photography and games
Moving-image media combine framing, editing, sound, performance, design and narrative. Games add rules, player agency, systems and interaction. Analyse representation, production conditions, accessibility, distribution and audience effects.

## Copyright and creative ethics
Copyright rules differ by country and treaty. Permission, licences, exceptions, public-domain status and attribution must be checked for the relevant jurisdiction and work. Giving credit does not automatically grant permission. Respect privacy, consent, cultural heritage and community ownership.

## Known gaps
Regional art histories, music traditions, art techniques, design disciplines, film history, game studies, sports history and country-specific copyright need dedicated articles.
