# Global Reference and Primary-Source Directory

This directory points readers toward organisations that publish international standards, data, research or official guidance. It is a starting directory, not an endorsement of every statement or a guarantee that every dataset is free to redistribute. Check the source's methodology, publication date, licence and geographic coverage. OUTPOST itself does not contact these sites; links require an internet connection.

## Health and life sciences
- World Health Organization (WHO): https://www.who.int/ — global health guidance, disease information and health statistics.
- US National Institutes of Health (NIH): https://www.nih.gov/ — biomedical research and health information; US-based and not a substitute for local guidance.
- US Centers for Disease Control and Prevention (CDC): https://www.cdc.gov/ — public-health resources; jurisdiction-specific guidance.
- World Organisation for Animal Health (WOAH): https://www.woah.org/ — animal health and zoonoses.
- Food and Agriculture Organization (FAO): https://www.fao.org/ — food, agriculture, fisheries and food security.

## Law, rights and international affairs
- United Nations: https://www.un.org/ — UN institutions, documents and international cooperation.
- UN Treaty Collection: https://treaties.un.org/ — treaty status and records.
- Office of the UN High Commissioner for Human Rights: https://www.ohchr.org/ — human-rights instruments and reporting.
- International Court of Justice: https://www.icj-cij.org/ — principal judicial organ of the UN.
- International Labour Organization: https://www.ilo.org/ — labour standards and work statistics.
- World Intellectual Property Organization: https://www.wipo.int/ — intellectual-property treaties, policy and resources.
- World Trade Organization: https://www.wto.org/ — trade agreements, disputes and trade data.
- International Maritime Organization: https://www.imo.org/ — international shipping safety and environmental rules.

## Economics, finance and development
- World Bank: https://www.worldbank.org/ and https://data.worldbank.org/ — development research and indicators.
- International Monetary Fund: https://www.imf.org/ — macroeconomic analysis and country data.
- OECD: https://www.oecd.org/ — comparative policy research and statistics, with membership and partner coverage limitations.
- Bank for International Settlements: https://www.bis.org/ — central-bank cooperation and financial stability.
- UN Trade and Development (UNCTAD): https://unctad.org/ — trade, investment and development.
- National statistical offices, central banks, securities regulators and official stock exchanges are the primary sources for country-specific figures and rules.

## Science, environment and space
- Intergovernmental Panel on Climate Change (IPCC): https://www.ipcc.ch/ — climate assessment reports.
- World Meteorological Organization (WMO): https://wmo.int/ — weather, climate and water coordination.
- UN Environment Programme (UNEP): https://www.unep.org/ — environmental assessment and policy.
- UNESCO: https://www.unesco.org/ — education, science, culture and heritage.
- NASA: https://www.nasa.gov/ — space science and exploration; US agency.
- European Space Agency (ESA): https://www.esa.int/ — European cooperative space programmes.
- US Geological Survey (USGS): https://www.usgs.gov/ — earth science and hazards; US agency.

## Computing, internet and technical standards
- Internet Engineering Task Force (IETF): https://www.ietf.org/ — internet protocol standards (RFCs).
- World Wide Web Consortium (W3C): https://www.w3.org/ — web standards and accessibility.
- Internet Corporation for Assigned Names and Numbers (ICANN): https://www.icann.org/ — domain name and identifier coordination.
- National Institute of Standards and Technology (NIST): https://www.nist.gov/ — measurement, cybersecurity and technical standards; US agency.
- International Telecommunication Union (ITU): https://www.itu.int/ — international telecommunications standards and statistics.
- International Organization for Standardization (ISO): https://www.iso.org/ — international standards; many standards are copyrighted and paid.

## Education, culture and heritage
- UNESCO Institute for Statistics: https://uis.unesco.org/ — education, science and culture indicators.
- International Council of Museums (ICOM): https://icom.museum/ — museum practice and ethics.
- International Council on Monuments and Sites (ICOMOS): https://www.icomos.org/ — cultural heritage conservation.
- World Intellectual Property Organization (WIPO): https://www.wipo.int/ — copyright and related rights.

## How to use sources responsibly
1. Prefer the primary law, dataset, standard or technical document for exact requirements.
2. Confirm the publisher, date, version, definitions and update frequency.
3. Check whether the source covers the user's country, population or industry.
4. Read methodology notes, caveats, corrections and licensing terms.
5. Cross-check high-impact claims with independent reputable sources.
6. Keep offline copies labelled with retrieval date; do not describe them as current after they become stale.
7. Standards and full legal texts may be copyrighted; link to official access rather than republishing without permission.

## Coverage gaps
This is a selected international directory, not a complete list of national agencies, universities, professional bodies, archives or open datasets. Country-by-country official source indexes remain a future expansion task.
