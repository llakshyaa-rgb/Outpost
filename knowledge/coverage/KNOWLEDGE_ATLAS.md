# OUTPOST Global Knowledge Atlas

This atlas is the coverage map for the built-in library. It is deliberately honest: a topic marked **partial** has a useful starting guide but is not a complete reference; **planned** means the subject is mapped but needs dedicated material. The world is too large for any finite library to promise literally zero gaps. This map makes omissions visible and gives every expansion a place to belong.

## Coverage states
- **covered** — a substantive guide exists; still subject to review.
- **partial** — introductory material exists; important subtopics remain.
- **planned** — mapped but no dedicated guide yet.
- **dynamic** — needs dated updates because facts, laws, prices, standards or guidance change.
- **audit** — check the article, links, terminology, accessibility and regional balance before upgrading status.

## Global scope rules
1. Explain general principles first, then compare regions and jurisdictions. Never present one country's law, currency, health system or custom as universal.
2. Label jurisdiction, date, units and uncertainty. For legal topics identify the country/state/province and distinguish statute, regulation, court decision and common practice.
3. Separate stable knowledge from dynamic facts. Store last-reviewed dates and official source links for dynamic material.
4. Include alternate names, spelling variants, common misspellings and plain-language search terms.
5. Distinguish evidence, consensus, disputed claims and speculation. Explain source quality.
6. Include accessibility, safety, ethics, privacy and environmental impacts where relevant.
7. Mark gaps rather than pretending a short article covers an entire discipline.

## Search vocabulary policy
Each article should include a **Search terms** line with synonyms, alternate spellings, abbreviations and everyday phrasing. Include sensitive or colloquial terms only where relevant and educational. Search should not depend on users guessing the article's formal title.

## Maintenance and gap review
- Run `python scripts/audit_knowledge.py` after adding or changing articles.
- Review all **planned**, **partial** and **dynamic** entries; do not silently mark them complete.
- Add a new topic to this atlas before writing its article so it is not orphaned.
- Review fast-changing legal, medical, financial, standards and technology pages at least annually or when authoritative guidance changes.
- The atlas is a map, not a substitute for primary sources, clinicians, qualified legal advice or current official data.
