# Engineering and Industry: Global Primer

**Coverage:** introductory guide. Engineering codes, electrical standards, building regulations and workplace rules are jurisdiction-specific; do not use this guide as a construction or repair specification.

**Search terms:** engineering, industry, manufacturing, civil engineering, mechanical engineering, electrical engineering, electronics, chemical engineering, materials, robotics, energy, construction, design, maintenance.

## Engineering method
Engineering applies science, mathematics and practical judgement to design systems that meet requirements under constraints. A typical process identifies needs, defines requirements, explores concepts, analyses risks, prototypes, tests, documents and maintains the result. Safety factors, failure analysis, accessibility, environmental effects and lifecycle cost matter.

## Major disciplines
- **Civil:** buildings, bridges, transport, water, geotechnics and infrastructure.
- **Mechanical:** machines, mechanisms, thermal systems, fluids and manufacturing.
- **Electrical:** power generation, transmission, distribution, motors and protection.
- **Electronics and communications:** circuits, semiconductors, sensors, signals and networks.
- **Chemical and process:** reaction systems, separation, process control and industrial safety.
- **Materials:** metals, polymers, ceramics, composites and material testing.
- **Industrial and systems:** operations, quality, logistics, reliability and human factors.
- **Software, control and robotics:** embedded systems, automation, sensing and feedback.

## Energy systems
Energy systems include fossil fuels, nuclear power, hydroelectricity, wind, solar, geothermal and bioenergy. Compare dispatchability, storage, grid integration, cost, emissions, land and water impacts, reliability and supply chains. No technology is impact-free.

## Manufacturing and quality
Manufacturing transforms materials through processes such as casting, machining, forming, joining, additive manufacturing and chemical processing. Quality systems use specifications, measurement, traceability, inspection and continuous improvement. Maintenance strategies include preventive, predictive and corrective work.

## Safety and standards
Engineering hazards include electrical shock, fire, pressure, toxic exposure, moving machinery, structural failure and heat. Use applicable local codes and recognised standards, competent supervision and certified equipment. Never treat an online explanation as approval to perform high-risk work.

## Known gaps
Detailed discipline textbooks, engineering calculations, standards catalogues, industrial process guides and country-specific licensing remain to be developed.
