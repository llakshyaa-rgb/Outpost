# Sports, Games and Recreation

**Coverage:** global introduction. Rules, competition formats, governing bodies and athlete eligibility can change; consult the relevant sport federation and event organiser.

**Search terms:** sports, sport, athletics, games, recreation, football, soccer, cricket, basketball, tennis, badminton, swimming, running, esports, exercise science, training, rules, tournaments.

## What sport includes
Sport includes organised competition, informal play, physical activity, mind sports and increasingly digital competition. Activities differ in physical demands, equipment, rules, team size, accessibility and cultural meaning. Recreation can be valuable without competition or elite performance.

## Major categories
- **Team field and court sports:** association football (soccer), rugby, field hockey, cricket, basketball, volleyball, handball and many regional games.
- **Racket and precision sports:** tennis, badminton, table tennis, squash, golf, archery, shooting sports and bowling.
- **Athletics and endurance:** running, jumping, throwing, road racing, cycling, triathlon and race walking.
- **Aquatic and outdoor:** swimming, diving, rowing, sailing, surfing, climbing, skiing and skating.
- **Combat sports:** wrestling, boxing, judo, taekwondo, fencing and other traditions with distinct rules and safety standards.
- **Mind and board games:** chess, go, bridge and other strategy competitions.
- **Digital competition:** esports and competitive gaming, with platform-specific rules and governance.

## Rules and governance
Each sport has rules defining play, scoring, equipment, officials, safety and eligibility. International federations, national associations, leagues, schools and event organisers may have different regulations. Rules can change; verify the current rulebook for a particular competition. Anti-doping systems use prohibited lists, testing and due-process procedures; athletes need current guidance from their governing body.

## Training and sports science
Training develops skills, strength, endurance, speed, mobility, coordination and decision-making. Progressive overload must be balanced with recovery, sleep, nutrition and injury prevention. Training should be adapted to age, experience, health, disability and environment. Pain, concussion symptoms, heat illness and serious injuries require appropriate medical assessment. Avoid extreme training or unverified performance-enhancing substances.

## Fair play, inclusion and safety
Fair play includes respect, honesty and rule compliance. Sport governance must address harassment, abuse, discrimination, match-fixing, corruption, unsafe facilities and conflicts of interest. Accessibility includes adapted rules, equipment, venues and communication. Participation opportunities differ by region, income, gender, disability and infrastructure.

## Spectatorship and data
Scores, rankings, schedules, records and athlete statistics are time-sensitive. Record event, season, ruleset, measurement method and source when comparing performances. Historical records may not be comparable across equipment, conditions or rule changes.
