WORLD LAW REFERENCE · NOT INDIA-ONLY

Legal material will be organised by jurisdiction and legal system, not by the user's country. Coverage will be layered: international instruments; regional systems; then national and subnational law. Availability and update dates vary by jurisdiction, so every legal item must retain its source, jurisdiction and last-checked date.

WorldLIIWorldwide legal research gateway: legislation, cases, treaties and country/region databases. UN Treaty CollectionInternational treaties, depositary status, parties, reservations and treaty texts. FAOLEXNational and regional laws and policies on food, agriculture and natural resources across 300+ jurisdictions. ILO NORMLEX / NATLEXInternational labour standards and national labour and social-security legislation. WIPO LexIntellectual-property legislation, treaties and country profiles. Library of Congress · Guide to Law OnlineCountry-by-country links to government and legal information sources.

Important: no single database contains every current law from every country. OUTPOST will distinguish official primary law from research guides and secondary summaries; it will not present an incomplete archive as complete legal advice.

DESTRUCTIVE ACTION

Removes OUTPOST database records, local indexes, imported knowledge packs, Wikipedia data and images, saved ZIM archives inside OUTPOST, and the generated Kiwix reader. Your original files in external folders are not deleted.

BACKUP & PRIVACY

Your data stays yours

Database: data/outpost.db
Configuration: data/config.json
Server: 127.0.0.1

The JSON export saves notes, collections and bookmarks only. It does not include files, ZIM archives, encyclopedia data, images or imported knowledge packs. Copy those separately before a full reset if you need them.
